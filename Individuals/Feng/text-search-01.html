Sources

Meier, Auszug aus der Vernunftlehre (1752; AA XVI), all 563 §§, read from the Meier reader's own text (meier-reader/meier-reading-guide.html), with the AA page each § is cited from.

Kant's Reflexionen on Meier (AA XVI, Refl. 1619–3488), from data/reflexionen-16-meier.js, grouped as the AA groups them by §§.

Logik Blomberg (ca. 1771; AA 24:7–301) and Logik Busolt (ca. 1789/90; AA 24:603–686), from data/logik-blomberg-24.js and data/logik-busolt-24.js.

Nothing is copied: the three data/ files are loaded with <script src>. Meier's text is not in data/, so the page reads it from the Meier reader when it is served over the web (GitHub Pages, raw.githack, or python3 -m http.server). Opened straight from disk, the browser forbids that, and the other three texts are searched without it.

Attested vs. inferred

The texts, their spelling, the AA pages and manuscript pages, the editorial brackets (faded), the Reflexionen's phase expressions and locus notes, and Blomberg's § headings are all shown as the edition has them.

Marked as inferences: a hit found only by the spelling-variant rules is underlined rather than highlighted, with ≈ beside it in the results; a Reflexion whose § was interpolated has a dashed border and the tag interpolated §; a lecture paragraph the extractor joined on its own judgement has a dashed rule at its left.

Computed by this page: all counts, rates per 10,000 words, the distribution bars, the neighbouring words, the shared words between pinned passages, and the key words suggested for a Meier §.

Spelling variants

In that mode words are compared after folding: lower case; ß → ss; ä ö ü → a o u; th → t; dt → t; y → i; ck → k; c → z before e/i, otherwise k (but ch is kept); ie → i; initial j → i; h after a vowel and before a consonant dropped; doubled letters single. So Urtheil finds Urteil, seyn finds sein, Erkenntniß finds Erkenntniss, Critick finds Kritik.
