    What is a Porphyrian tree?

    In his Isagoge (c. 270 CE), the Neoplatonist Porphyry explained how a highest genus is divided by pairs of opposed differentiae into ever narrower species, down to individuals.

    Each step yields a definition by genus proximum plus differentia specifica. Human, for example, is defined as a mortal rational animal.

    The "tree" picture came later, through Boethius and medieval logic textbooks. The same idea lives on in biological taxonomy, library classification, and computer ontologies.
