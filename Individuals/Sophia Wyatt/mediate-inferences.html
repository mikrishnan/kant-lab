Mediate inferences in Kant’s logic lectures

Examples of mediate inferences (Vernunftschlüsse) in the Blomberg, Busolt and Wiener logic transcripts, each in its paragraph with the inference highlighted.

What is attested, what is not. The paragraph text, the page numbers (Academy Edition, AA XXIV) and the manuscript page numbers are the transcriptions’, as data/logik-*-24.js carries them; a grey tag such as AA 24:673 marks where a new page begins. Which stretch counts as a mediate inference, and the label on each, is the compiler’s reading, not the edition’s. The list is a curated selection from a read of the three transcripts, not an exhaustive census. Short notes are the compiler’s, not Kant’s or his students’.

Immediate inferences (Verstandesschlüsse: conversion, contraposition, subalternation and the like) are left out.
