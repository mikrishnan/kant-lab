Your division

A division splits one concept into narrower concepts. Name the concept, add its members, and list the things you take to fall under each. List the whole sphere of the divided concept too, so completeness can be tested.

How the tests work

Busolt a rule he states, quoted from the transcript. my test how I turned it into something a program can check.

The program does not know what falls under a concept. It compares only the lists you type, so it can show that your lists contradict a rule but not that your lists are right. Busolt gives no instance lists; the ones in the examples are mine. Whether “opposed” (entgegen) means exactly “no shared instance” is my reading, not his definition.
