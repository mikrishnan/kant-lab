Immediate Inferences drawn

Kant's examples of the consequentiae immediatae — inferences from one judgement to another with no middle term — from the Blomberg, Wiener and Busolt logic transcripts (AA 24) and from his own Reflexionen on Meier's Auszug (AA 16). Each passage stands beside a figure of what the inference does. The Academy Edition prints no figures: every drawing here is a reading of the passage, and marks what it supplies.

The list view, with the judgements side by side, is the Immediate Inferences explorer.

Kant sorts them by the four heads of the table of judgements: Die conseqventiae immediatae als unmittelbar, d.i. Verstandesschlüsse … gehen durch alle classen der categorien. Refl. 3170, AA 16:692

How to read the figures

Each circle is the sphere of a concept. Blomberg explains the forms of judgement this way: the subject is „gantz in der Sphaera des Praedicats enthalten“, „gantz außerhalb“, or „nur dem Theile nach“ (AA 24:275); Busolt the conversion of an A: „das Subiect Mensch ist unter der Sphäre der Sterblichen“ (AA 24:671). In a row of panels the drawing stays the same and only the shading moves, because an immediate inference is, in the Wiener transcript's words, „der Materie nach eins, nur Form verschieden“ (AA 24:938). The square sets the judgements out as Refl. 3177 does, universals above and particulars below. A green ✓ under a panel means the figure bears the judgement out; an amber ⚠ that the figure shows more than the premise says; a red ✕ that the judgement picks out an empty region, or that the figure contradicts it.

Attested and supplied. The passage's own words are in solid boxes and are quoted exactly; a letter in a solid box is a form the passage names, in a dashed box a form read off the sentence. Dashed panels, circles and lines are supplied here, not in the passage. The short concept names over each figure are the annotator's, and so is which judgement is read as subject and predicate. Pages are the Academy pages, worked out on load from the shared transcript data, which also confirms every quotation.
