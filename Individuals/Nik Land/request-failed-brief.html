Why it keeps saying “request failed”

Executive brief · 25 October 2026 · about the Claude session that built the diagram panel

Bottom line

It isn't the Kant Lab code. Nothing in the repository says “request failed”. The message comes from the Claude app: its full text is API Error: Connection dropped (ECONNRESET). It means the connection to Claude's servers was cut while a reply was still arriving.

Every cut came after about six minutes. Six replies were cut off today, each between 6 min 3 s and 6 min 38 s in. Every reply that took less than six minutes arrived. The session that kept failing had grown very large over nine days (537,000 tokens, with 8 full-size screenshots). The whole conversation is sent again with every reply, so its long steps ran close to six minutes, and some ran past it.

“Try again” can't help, because it repeats the same long step. Each retry ran the same six minutes and was cut at the same point. The fix is to work in a fresh session and in smaller steps. This session is a fresh one, and the work here is being done that way.

Why that session's replies were slow

What it is not, and what's still unknown

Ruled out

  • Your pages. No file in the repository prints the message.
  • Your internet being down. Short replies arrived between the cut-off ones, e.g. 13:34 and 13:35.
  • The Mac sleeping, for five of the six. The power log shows the Mac awake through those five. It went to sleep about 35 s after each cut, because the app stops holding it awake once a reply has failed.
  • Lost work. Files the session had already saved are still on disk: the diagram changes to immediate-inferences.html are there, uncommitted.

Contributing, or unknown

  • The lid, once. At 13:36 the lid was closed mid-step. The next reply was sent during a brief wake and died while the Mac slept. That is the 31-minute bar.
  • Who cuts at six minutes. From this Mac I can't see whether it's the Wi-Fi network or the service end that closes long connections. One reply on 23 October ran 7 min 24 s and arrived, so it isn't a fixed limit. Either way, the remedy is the same.
  • Battery. The Mac ran from 29% down to 18% on battery during the failures. Plugging in doesn't fix the six-minute cuts (two came after 14:05, on power), but it does stop sleep.

What to do

When you see it

  1. Don't press “Try again” more than once. If the retry fails too, it will keep failing.
  2. Type “continue, in smaller steps” instead, so the next reply is short.
  3. If a session has run for days, start a new one for the next piece of work. Uncommitted files carry over, and a short note on what's done is enough to pick up from.
  4. Keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open while a long task runs.

What I'm doing differently in this session

  • Fresh session, so each reply re-sends far less: about 100k tokens now, against 537k.
  • The new page is written in several modest pieces, so no single reply comes near six minutes.
  • Screenshots are shrunk before I look at them, and the heavy checking runs in scripts, not in the conversation.
