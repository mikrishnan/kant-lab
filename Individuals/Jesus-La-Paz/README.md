# Jesus La Paz

Personal sandbox for Jesus La Paz (@yizu6201).

- [logik-search.html](logik-search.html): one-page app for reading and searching the Blomberg, Busolt and Wiener logic transcripts. Tabs on the left hold the text; the panel on the right searches a term and shows it in context. Reads the shared files in `data/`.
- [meier-kant-comparison.html](meier-kant-comparison.html): Meier's *Auszug* against Kant. **Connections** shows, for a searched word, each Meier § beside the passages of the Blomberg, Wiener and Busolt transcripts, the Jäsche *Logik* and Kant's Reflexionen that lecture on it; **By §** and **By term** read and search the texts. Every link says how it is known (stated by the text, stated by the Academy Edition, or inferred, with a confidence).
- [CONNECTION-RULES.md](CONNECTION-RULES.md): the rules by which passages are connected to Meier's §§, how they were tested, and their limits.
- [meier-kant-concordance.js](meier-kant-concordance.js): the links themselves, generated by [build-concordance.py](build-concordance.py) from the texts in the repository; rebuild after any of them changes.
- [jaesche-logik-data.js](jaesche-logik-data.js) (from `Textfiles/Vol9Logic.rtfd` by [parse-jaesche.py](parse-jaesche.py)) and [meier-auszug-data.js](meier-auszug-data.js) (Meier's text, copied from the Meier reader): data for the comparison, kept here until `data/` has files for them.
