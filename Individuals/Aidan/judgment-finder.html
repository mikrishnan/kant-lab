KANT'S FORMS OF JUDGMENT IN THE LOGIC LECTURES

Type words that occur in a judgment (German, any spelling). Each example found is labelled with its forms.
An example is a sentence the lecturer introduces with z.E., z.B. or zum Beispiel. Form labels are inferred from the wording of the sentence — quantifiers, negation, wenn…so, entweder…oder, muß, kann — and can be wrong; the German is the transcription's, the English is machine translation.
