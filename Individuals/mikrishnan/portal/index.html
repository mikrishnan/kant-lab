How to ask

falsch finds the word in every spelling the texts use: Urtheil and Urteil, Wiederspruch and Widerspruch, Principium and Prinzip, Saz and Satz, are one word to the search. fals* finds every word that begins so. Several words, form* fals*, find places where they stand within the chosen number of words of each other, in any order. Quotation marks, "der form nach", keep words together and in order. OR joins alternatives. A regular expression between slashes, /formali?ter/, is matched against the text as printed.

The Phrasings table lists every distinct wording the search found, with how often each occurs in each text. Click a phrasing to see only its passages. Click a passage to open it on the right, with the textbook § it belongs to, Kant's notes there, and the other texts on the same §.
