falsch finds the word in every spelling the texts use: Urtheil and Urteil, Wiederspruch and Widerspruch, Principium and Prinzip, Saz and Satz, are one word to the search. fals* finds every word that begins so. Several words, form* fals*, find places where they stand near each other, in any order. Quotation marks, "der form nach", keep words together and in order. OR joins alternatives. A regular expression between slashes, /formali?ter/, is matched against the text as printed.

Overview shows every distinct phrasing the search found and where it occurs; Passages lists them. Click a passage to read it in its full text on the right; Connections there shows the textbook § it belongs to, Kant’s notes on it, the other texts on the same §, and its parallel passages. Select words in any text to search for them.
