Logik Busolt

Immanuel Kant, lectures on logic, as taken down by G. C. W. Busolt, Königsberg 1790
Akademie-Ausgabe XXIV, 603–686 · German with facing English translation

The German is the repository's transcription of AA XXIV, with the Academy Edition's page numbers in brackets where each page begins, and the manuscript's own pagination as small raised numerals (German side only). The student's spelling and slips are kept. In the English, each bracketed page number stands where the corresponding German page break falls; its position is the translator's alignment, not the edition's. A paragraph with a dashed rule beside it was joined by the extractor on its own judgement, with no marker in the transcription. Bracketed notes marked "tr." are the translator's.
