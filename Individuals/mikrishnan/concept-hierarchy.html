Concept Hierarchy Builder

At the top stands the concept of an object in general, the empty concept: it has no marks, and every other concept lies beneath it. Add concepts beneath it, one by one. A concept is individuated by its intension — the higher concepts above it — and its extension is the concepts beneath it. Click any concept in the tree to read the truths that hold of it in virtue of how the tree was built.

Selected concept: — click another concept in the tree to select it.

Separate several with commas. Enter adds beneath; Shift+Enter adds at the same level. Inserting puts the new concept directly beneath the selected one, with everything that lay beneath the selected concept now in the new concept’s extension; several names make a chain, the first highest. Each concept may appear only once in the hierarchy.

the concept of an object in general (the empty concept) selected concept its marks (intension: the higher concepts) its extension (the lower concepts)

Drag a concept onto another to put it, with everything beneath it, in that concept’s extension.
