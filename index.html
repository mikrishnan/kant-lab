Kant Lab

Digital-humanities tools for reading the pre-Kantian German school philosophy that Kant lectured from.

For the seminar

  • The Logic Portal

    Ask the whole logic corpus a question at once: Meier, Baumgarten, the Reflexionen, the Blomberg, Wiener and Busolt lectures, and Jäsche, in every spelling. Any passage leads to the Meier § it is on, Kant's dated notes there and the other lectures on it. Built from the seminar's own tools.

    Its number (Refl. 1619–6455) or its page in the Academy edition. It opens in the Logic Portal, beside the textbook § it is on.
  • The Method Log

    A record of how the lab works, session by session: the ideas each meeting discussed, what was tried in the portal or with AI, what came of it, and whether it worked. Paste a summary or a transcript and it sorts into ideas; the verdicts stay yours.

Retired on 4 November 2026: the Baumgarten and Meier Readers, whose textbooks are now read in the Logic Portal with Kant’s notes beside them (their old addresses redirect there), and the Porphyrian Tree, one of the seminar’s early experiments.
