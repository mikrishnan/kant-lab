Kant Lab

Digital-humanities tools for reading the pre-Kantian German school philosophy that Kant lectured from.

For the seminar

  • The Logic Portal

    Ask the whole logic corpus a question at once: Meier, Baumgarten, the Reflexionen, the Blomberg, Wiener and Busolt lectures, and Jäsche, in every spelling. Any passage leads to the Meier § it is on, Kant's dated notes there and the other lectures on it. Built from the seminar's own tools.

    Its number (Refl. 1619–6455) or its page in the Academy edition. It opens in the Logic Portal, beside the textbook § it is on.

Tools

  • Baumgarten Reader

    Latin reading guide for Baumgarten's Metaphysica, with inline German glosses.

  • Meier Reader

    German reading guide for Meier's Auszug aus der Vernunftlehre, with a side panel of parallel passages from the tradition.

  • Porphyrian Tree

    A configurator for building genus–species trees under user-chosen division rules.
