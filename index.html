Kant Lab

For the seminar

  • The Logic Portal

    Ask the whole logic corpus a question: Meier, Baumgarten, the Reflexionen, the Blomberg, Wiener and Busolt lectures, and Jäsche, in every spelling. Any passage leads to the Meier § it is on, Kant's dated notes there and the other lectures on it. Built from the seminar's own tools.

Tools

Being folded into the Logic Portal, and phased out here.

  • Baumgarten Reader

    Latin reading guide for Baumgarten's Metaphysica, with inline German glosses.

  • Meier Reader

    German reading guide for Meier's Auszug aus der Vernunftlehre, with a side panel of parallel passages from the tradition.

  • Porphyrian Tree

    A configurator for building genus–species trees under user-chosen division rules.
