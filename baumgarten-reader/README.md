# Baumgarten Reader (retired)

Retired on 4 November 2026. Everything it did — the *Metaphysica* with its German glosses, misprint notes, 1757 pages and Synopsis, and the Reflexionen beside each §, with those that belong to no § — is now in the
[Logic Portal](../logic-portal/index.html#read=baumgarten), which reads the same data from
[`../data/`](../data/). Open Baumgarten’s *Metaphysica* there under **Read**.

The address still works. [baumgartenreading-guide.html](baumgartenreading-guide.html) redirects into the portal, and a link to a
§ (`#go=92`), a page of the Academy edition (`#go=16:236`) or a paragraph (`#para-92`)
opens the same place there.

Sophia Wyatt (@sophia-wyatt) and Maria (@mari637-pixel) looked after it, and still look
after the data it was built from. The reader as it last stood, with its spec and its
notes for Claude, is at commit
[`9d47366`](https://github.com/mikrishnan/kant-lab/tree/9d473662a1a2a947232aed780ffbd180e9f233c0/baumgarten-reader);
[open it as it was](https://raw.githack.com/mikrishnan/kant-lab/9d473662a1a2a947232aed780ffbd180e9f233c0/baumgarten-reader/baumgartenreading-guide.html).
