# The class screen

A handout on the room's screen: its sections, its notes and its passages in large type, one after
another, with the English beneath them as the handout has it. Open it with **Class screen ↗** on the
portal's Handouts page. It opens in a window of its own, which you can drag onto the room's screen
and make full screen, while the portal stays on your laptop.

**The class password.** The screen shows nothing until it is given, as the Handouts page does;
with **Remember on this computer** it is not asked again in that browser.

**What it shows.** By default, the handout open on the Handouts page in this browser, published or
not. It follows that handout as you work. A passage or note you add in the portal appears on the
screen, and the screen keeps its place. If you open another handout there, the screen shows that
one, from its top. From the menu at the top left you can choose any handout in this browser
instead, or one published for the class, or open a file saved with the Handouts page's **Save to
file**. A published handout also opens directly at `class-screen/?handout=<name>`, and its page in
the portal has an **On the class screen** link.

**How a handout shows.** Its section headings stand as headings. A note, the point to make, has a
number, and the passages under it are numbered beneath it (3.1, 3.2). Each passage keeps its label
and its note, and the highlights and underlines you gave it, with […] for any words you left out; a paragraph of a note that opens **Answer:** is folded under
**Answer ▸** until you open it, and the list of items at the top leaves it out; your margin notes are yours and are
not shown. An excerpt shows the words you chose, and a joined passage its parts, each with its
citation.

**Moving through it**

| Key | |
| --- | --- |
| ↓, →, Space, Page Down | on: through a long passage a screen at a time, then to the next item. A folded answer in view opens first |
| ↑, ←, Shift-Space, Page Up | back |
| Home, End | the first item, the last |
| E | English: each translation style in turn, then none. It is on until you choose otherwise, and a passage whose English the handout hides shows none here either |
| N | the notes on passages, shown or hidden |
| C | the passage in context: the portal beside it, opened at that passage |
| F | full screen. The bar fades after a moment and comes back when the mouse moves |
| + and − | larger or smaller type |

The menu in the middle of the bar goes straight to any item. Clicking an item makes it the one in
hand.

**What it says about the texts.** The English is a machine translation following one published
translator's method, and the screen says so above each one, as the portal does. A passage from a
text that is OCR carries the text's measured share of words right. A Reflexion's dating is Adickes'
phase, with the portal's years beside it.

**How it works.** The screen loads the portal itself, hidden, and reads the passages and the English
from it, so it shows exactly what the portal shows. It reads the handouts as the portal keeps them,
and changes neither. It therefore has to be opened from the same site as the portal, on GitHub
Pages or a local server, not as a file. The first opening takes as long as the portal does to load.
