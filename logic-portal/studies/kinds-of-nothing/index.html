The kinds of nothing

What are the kinds of nothing — the nihil negativum and privativum, the ens rationis and imaginarium — in Baumgarten and in Kant, and how and why does Kant’s account change?

A study: a sandbox for building tools for this one question, beside the Logic Portal and drawing on it. The portal itself is unchanged by anything here. Whatever you open shows on the right in the portal itself, so its full text, Compare and Connections are all there.

  • It shows; it never says. No summaries, and no “Kant’s view”.
  • Dates as the edition gives them: Adickes’ phases verbatim, query marks and all. Where this page places a note in time, the placing is marked as its own.
  • A passage is in the dossier only because a participant put it there, and says why.
