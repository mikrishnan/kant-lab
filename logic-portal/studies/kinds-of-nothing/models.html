Three models of nothing

A draft, for the seminar to correct. Each model has the same fields. The shaded fields are Kant’s words, cut from the portal’s own text of each passage, with its citation; click a citation to read the passage where it stands. The dashed fields are the seminar’s to state: what is divided, by what, the model’s problems, and what it does about earlier ones. They are empty until someone fills and signs them.
