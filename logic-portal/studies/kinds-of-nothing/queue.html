The queue

Passages from the portal’s search, numbered, for the seminar to flag against Kant’s table of nothing. Tick every kind a passage bears on, or None. Ticks are saved to GitHub as you make them, signed with your GitHub name. Everyone working on the queue sees them within about half a minute. The page never ticks anything itself.
